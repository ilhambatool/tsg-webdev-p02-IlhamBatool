FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before planning your special event with us.

How far in advance should I book an event?

We recommend booking as early as possible, especially for weddings and large celebrations. This gives us enough time to plan every detail.

Do you provide decoration and styling?

Yes. Our services can include event décor, styling, florals, tablescapes, lighting, and other decorative details.

Can I customize an event package?

Absolutely. Our packages can be customized according to your event type, guest count, theme, venue, and requirements.

Do you work with outside vendors?

Yes. We can coordinate with vendors you have already selected or help manage vendors as part of your event planning package.

Do you handle weddings and large events?

Yes. We plan and coordinate everything from intimate celebrations to weddings and larger events with detailed planning requirements.

Do you travel to different cities?

Yes. We can plan events in different locations. Travel arrangements and additional costs can be discussed during the consultation.
